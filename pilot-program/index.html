    Pilot Partner Program

    Yozi Systems Labs is a founder-led, technically focused laboratory. We partner with industry evaluators to integrate the Vectra™ platform into specialized diagnostic and simulation pipelines.

    Evaluation Path

    We provide multiple pathways for organizations evaluating finite-state stochastic-process workloads.

    1. Vectra Diagnostic Review

    An early-stage technical audit where you submit representative workloads for structural and numerical analysis. This is often the most efficient way to confirm the Vectra Difference on your own models. - Learn more about Diagnostic Reviews

    2. Vectra Evaluation SDK

    For teams ready to embed the runtime locally, we provide an evaluation bundle under our Pilot Program. 1. Exploration: Review the Vectra SDK documentation. 2. Request Evaluation: Contact us at info@yozi.systems. 3. Onboarding: Qualified partners receive a Vectra Evaluation SDK for their target architecture. 4. Support: Receive direct engineering support during the integration and verification phase.

    Why Partner with Us?

    • Technically Focused: We prioritize the solver engine's integrity and integration ergonomics over corporate overhead.
    • Lean & Responsive: Direct access to the engineers who built the ATGS Enterprise Core.
    • Evaluator-Oriented: We understand the requirements of high-stakes diagnostic environments.
