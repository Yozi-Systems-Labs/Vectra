    Vectra Diagnostic Review

    As part of our commitment to technical excellence and evaluator trust, Yozi Systems Labs offers the Vectra Diagnostic Review—an early-stage technical evaluation service for organizations developing complex finite-state stochastic models.

    Early diagnostic reviews may be available for qualified organizations evaluating finite-state stochastic-process workloads.

    Purpose

    The Vectra Diagnostic Review is a structured technical assessment designed to help evaluators understand the numerical and structural properties of their models using the Vectra™ validation engine. This is a selective, founder-led service focused on technical verification, not open-ended consulting or advisory.

    What You Can Submit

    Organizations may submit representative samples of their stochastic workloads for review, including: - Transition Matrices: CTMC generator matrices in dense or sparse formats. - Stochastic Models: Finite-state process definitions and graph-based models. - CTMC Workloads: Production-style workloads requiring first-passage or transient analysis. - Diagnostic Cases: Models suspected of numerical instability or stiffness. - Evaluation Inputs: Benchmark-style inputs for performance and precision testing.

    Analysis Scope

    Leveraging the ATGS Enterprise Core runtime, we analyze your submission for: - Validation & Refusal Behavior: How the model interacts with high-integrity validation gates. - Matrix Integrity: Detailed audit of row-sum invariants and off-diagonal discipline. - Structural Classification: Analysis of absorbing, transient, and irreducible components. - Computability: Verification of first-passage and transient solver preconditions. - Numerical Risk: Identification of stiffness, ill-conditioning, and residual errors. - Integration Readiness: Assessment of how the model structure aligns with the Vectra SDK.

    What You Receive

    Following the review, you will receive a Structured Diagnostic Report containing: - Model Risk Audit: Identified numerical or topological risks. - Refusal Findings: Detailed explanation of any refusal or error codes triggered. - Numerical Observations: Insights into stiffness ratios and condition estimates. - Integration Recommendations: Suggested paths for embedding the runtime into your stack. - Next Evaluation Path: Recommendations for moving toward a full Pilot Program.

    Engagement Boundaries

    • Selectivity: We evaluate submissions based on technical fit and availability. Acceptance for a review is not guaranteed.
    • Commercial Intent: A diagnostic review is an evaluation tool and does not constitute a guarantee of a long-term commercial engagement.
    • No Production Claims: Reports are for evaluation purposes only and do not constitute a claim of production readiness or regulated certification.
    • No Professional Advice: Vectra Diagnostic Reviews do not provide legal, financial, medical, or safety certification advice.
