    Platform & Capabilities

    Vectra™ is a specialized runtime platform for the exact analysis of continuous-time Markov chains (CTMCs) and complex graph-based processes, powered by the ATGS Enterprise Core engine.

    High-Integrity Validation Architecture

    Vectra implements a unique "Validation-Refusal" architecture. Rather than producing potentially corrupted results on unstable or invalid models, Vectra rigorously validates every input.

    • Validation Discipline: Enforces row-sum invariants and off-diagonal non-negativity with extreme precision.
    • Structured Refusal: When a model fails validation, Vectra provides a programmatic refusal report, allowing calling systems to diagnose the failure without crashing.

    Advanced Solver Suite

    Vectra delivers exact numerical solutions where traditional simulations often fail to converge or provide sufficient precision.

    First-Passage Analysis

    Compute exact mean first-passage times (MFPT) and variances for absorbing processes. Vectra handles complex state-spaces to identify process completion probabilities and temporal reliability.

    Exact Transient Computation

    Calculate probability distributions at arbitrary time horizons using high-order uniformization. The underlying ATGS engine manages Poisson truncation and convergence tracking automatically.

    Stiffness Diagnostics

    Vectra identifies "stiff" models—those with extreme exit-rate disparities—and provides diagnostic flags to ensure the numerical integrity of the results is maintained.

    Embeddable Runtime Architecture

    The Vectra platform is delivered as an embeddable SDK, centered around the stable ATGS C ABI. This ensures that the high-performance C++20 runtime can be integrated into nearly any environment—from Python-based research pipelines to production C/C++ diagnostic systems—without exposing internal implementation details.
